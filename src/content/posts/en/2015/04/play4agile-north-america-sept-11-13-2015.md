Join the fun at Play4Agile North America Sept 11th to 13th, 2015 at YMCA Cedar Glen in Schomberg, Ontario, Canada! Play4Agile is an all-inclusive, highly interactive community un-conference, an open playground for all to play and co-create transformative experiences.

Spawning from the very popular Play4Agile conference that happens in Ruckersbach, Germany each February, Play4Agile North America will bring together Agile practitioners – as well as others who are interested in using playful approaches to build stronger teams – for 2 days of Open Space on sharing and creating new games to accelerate learning and get work done.  All kinds of ideas germinate at Play4Agile: at past events, participants have created, play-tested and shared many games.   The after-hours camaraderie is a vital part of the experience as the conversations and creativity extend into the wee hours.

Come share, learn, connect and play!

More details and registration information at play4agilenorthamerica.wordpress.com
