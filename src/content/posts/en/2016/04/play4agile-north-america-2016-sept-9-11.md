Join us at Play4Agile North America 2016 for 3 days of fun and games!  This event spreads the magic of the very popular Play4Agile unconference to North America, bringing together Agile practitioners and others interested in creating and sharing playful ways of helping teams learn new skills and get work done in two days of Open Space.

Play4Agile North America 2016 will happen on Sept 9-11 2016 at the NAV Centre in Cornwall, Ontario, Canada, which is easy to get to from Montreal’s Trudeau International Airport.  Check out all the ways you can get to Cornwall here.

We expect the event to sell-out, so sign up today at Play4AgileNorthAmerica.com
