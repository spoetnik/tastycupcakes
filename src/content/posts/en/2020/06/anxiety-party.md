A workshop for expressing work anxieties and getting feedback. It helps to eliminate the worries you have and to look at other perspectives. An opportunity to learn a lot about your colleagues.

## Goal

-   Increase trust level within the team
-   Emotional pressure is released
-   The team is more synchronized about their differences and conflict situations

## Highlights

-   The workshop is useful for teams or people working together.
-   2-12 people.
-   1-3 hours.

## Source

? SoftSkillsPills.com – a soft skills platform for IT people where you play to learn.
