Virtual World is a simulation that allows students to experience Scrum in Virtual Classes. In this simulation, students are encouraged to build their favorite place ( a zoo, city, island, or tourism farm) in a Virtual canvas using the Scrum Framework. For more visit https://www.virtualscrumsimulation.com
