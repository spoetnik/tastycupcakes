**TastyCupcakes Community**

The games, techniques, and approaches presented are here to use and explore. All we ask is that you tell others about us and give us some feedback on the games themselves.

TastyCupcakes is a community-run site founded by [Michael McCullough](/about/michael-mccullough/ "Michael McCullough") and [Don McGreal](/about/don-mcgreal/ "Don McGreal") after they presented a series of games at Agile 2008 in Toronto.

If you have a passion for games and simulations, [join the community now.](/wp-login.php)

Please follow TastyCupcakes on Twitter!
And while you’re at it, follow Don and Mike.

The games, techniques and approaches presented are here to use and explore. All we ask is that you tell others about us and give us some feedback on the games themselves.
