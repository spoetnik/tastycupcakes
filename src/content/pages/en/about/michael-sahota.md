Michael is an Agile and Lean Coach, Consultant and Trainer at Agilitrix Inc located in Toronto, Canada. Michael has been using Agile for 10 years and makes a difference by helping individuals, teams and companies achieve their potential.

Michael has a M.Sc. from U.B.C. in Computer Science and is the only Certified Scrum Coach in Canada.

Michael has a passion for engaging learners through games and interactive workshops. Learn more about Michael by reading his blog.
